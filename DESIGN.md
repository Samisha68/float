# Float design system

Updated 6 September 2026 from the founder’s explicit brand correction. This supersedes the earlier blue palette and Ascending Arcade mark.

## Product

Short-term working capital against business invoices. The current interface serves business applicants and Float operators.

## Brand assets

Use the founder-supplied assets copied into `web/public/`:

- `float-favicon.svg`: white symbol on a black rounded background; browser icon and compact header mark.
- `float-logo.svg`: black symbol and outlined FLOAT lettering; full logo on white surfaces.

Source originals live in the separate Float_website project. Do not edit that project. Do not recreate the logo using text or use the retired arcade mark.

## Palette

Black (#000000) and white (#FFFFFF). Neutral grey (#737373) and its opacities may be used for secondary text, borders, and surfaces. No blue or other chromatic accent colors. Carry all statuses in explicit words, never red/amber/green.

## Typography and layout

Retain Poppins for the interface, weights 400–700. Use the supplied artwork for the logo. Tabular figures for money and dates. Spacing follows the 4px base scale; corners 6, 10, or 14px. Mobile layouts must remain usable without horizontal scrolling.

## Motion

Short ease-out transitions, 150–350ms. Respect reduced-motion preferences. No bounce or confetti.
